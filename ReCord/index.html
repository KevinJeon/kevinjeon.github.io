What is reactivity reliance?

Reactivity reliance is the dependence on other agents yielding instead of the ego adapting.

Truthful coordination emerges from the ego's adaptation, not other agents yielding.

Same scene, Same partner. Only reactivity changes. But the results are different!

When the partner reacts, it gets out of the way on its own.

When that partner is replayed, it will not move, so the ego has to yield.

01

Why the reactivity reliance is important

  1. The ego may learn to depend on other agents yielding instead of adapting itself.

    A conflict can often be resolved if either the ego or the partner yields. Reactive-PBT exposes the ego to diverse partners, but those partners still act online. If they repeatedly yield first, the ego may learn to depend on that response instead of learning when to yield itself.

  2. Unseen agents may not respond as expected

    Unseen other vehicles may react unexpectedly. Therefore, evaluation on not only whether the ego succeeds with an adaptive partner is needed, but what happens when the same kind of partner no longer adapts to the ego.

02

How ReCord mitigates the reactivity reliance

ReCord keeps the diversity of population-based training but changes how the partners are executed. Before ego training, it collects action traces from a population of pre-trained policies. During training, action traces are replayed instead of running their policies online. Thus, partner's actions can no longer change in response to the ego's current behavior, so the ego must learn to handle conflicts without depending on favorable partner adaptation.

Reactive-PBT

Partner policy runs online

Partners observe the situation and can yield, brake, or avoid the ego in response.

ReCord

Partner action trace replayed

Partners follow actions recorded in advance. The ego's current behavior does not change their next action.

03

Zero-shot coordination experiment setup

Robustness across diverse population and sampling

Population

ReCord is robust with diverse partner populations. L+N and Mix are predefined reward-based populations. ME is an unsupervised maximum-entropy population.

Sampling

ReCord is robust with diverse sampling methods. Uniform draws partners evenly. Curriculum and prioritized sampling are the structured alternatives.

Zero-shot partners

The same unseen partners are evaluated in two ways, reactive and non-reactive. A reactive partner chooses its actions online as the scene changes. A non-reactive partner follows an action trace recorded in advance.

US / USR

Unseen seeds. Partners use the same reward function as the training population but are trained with unseen random seeds. US evaluates them reactively, while USR uses their pre-recorded trajectories.

UR / URR

Unseen rewards. Partners are trained with reward functions unseen during ego training, creating behavioral mismatch. UR evaluates them reactively, while URR uses pre-recorded trajectories.

LR

Human log-replay. Partners follow recorded human trajectories, providing a natural non-reactive evaluation.

reactivity reliance gap is measured by the change in collision metrics (at-fault collision, collision rate) between the non-reactive and reactive evaluations (USR − US, or URR − UR). A smaller gap indicates less performance degradation when partner reactivity is removed, but it must be considered alongside collision and task-success performance in both modes.

Metric What it records
Collision count Collisions per agent.
Collision rate Episodes with a collision, as a percentage.
At-fault collision Collisions attributed to the ego, as a percentage.
Completion Episodes in which the ego reaches the goal.
Success Episodes in which the ego reaches the goal without a collision.

04

ReCord mitigates the reactivity reliance and improves zero-shot coordination in non-reactive evaluation

ReCord reduces the reactivity reliance gap across collision metrics while remaining competitive with Reactive-PBT under reactive evaluation with unseen partners. The reduction corresponds to fewer collisions under non-reactive evaluation, including human log-replay, while task completion is largely preserved.

ReCord reduces reactivity reliance

With the L+N population under uniform sampling, the reactivity reliance gap shrinks for both unseen seeds and unseen rewards. The values below are collision count.

Under reactive unseen-seed evaluation with uniform sampling, Reactive-PBT has slightly fewer collisions (0.090 vs. 0.102 per agent) and a slightly higher success rate (97.5% vs. 97.0%). Reactive evaluation is broadly comparable. The smaller gap should be read together with collision performance in both modes, rather than as a standalone improvement.

Better coordination with human log-replay

ReCord reduces collision count, collision rate, and at-fault collision relative to Reactive-PBT, while generally maintaining or improving completion and success. On log-replay, structured sampling further reduces ReCord's collision count. The same reduction is not observed consistently for Reactive-PBT.

05

Computational efficiency

Raw training throughput is measured over 100 million steps on one RTX 3090, for four seeds, under uniform, prioritized, and curriculum sampling.

126K vs 42K

Raw SPS · uniform sampling · single RTX 3090

ReCord reaches about 3× higher raw SPS across these strategies. Amortizing the one-time replay preparation over the 2 × 109-step budget leaves about 123K effective SPS under uniform sampling, 2.9× Reactive-PBT, with less than 3% overhead.
